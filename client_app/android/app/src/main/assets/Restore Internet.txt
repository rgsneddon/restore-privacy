Restore Internet — failsafe (Android)
=====================================

If residual VPN is stuck or you want complete removal:

1. Open the system VPN / notification shade and **Disconnect** any active
   Restore Privacy / Privacy Restored VPN session.
2. Settings → Network & internet → VPN → remove Restore Privacy if listed.
3. Settings → Apps → Restore Privacy → Uninstall
   (or long-press the app icon → Uninstall).
4. Optional: clear app storage before uninstall if the option is shown.

After uninstall, ordinary mobile/Wi‑Fi internet is restored and the product
is removed from the device.

Reinstall: download the current APK from https://restoreprivacy.online/ after
payment.
