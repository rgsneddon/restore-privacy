# rpOS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-01

rpOS (Restore Privacy Operating System) is designed for **user privacy**. Residual
networking and optional VPN features are product-configured; the free basic Rx
browser VPN extension is **IPv4-only** with no extra residual settings surface.

## What we minimise

- No unnecessary connection/session/user-info logs in residual node posture.
- Public status surfaces stay title-only where product policy requires it.
- Commercial deployments may process business contact data under customer contract
  only (moderator-controlled company email creation).

## Device wipe installer

RESTORE rpOS is a **destructive** install path on supported desktop platforms.
Users must receive an explicit warning before wipe. Mobile platforms follow
sideload / MDM honesty — see README.
