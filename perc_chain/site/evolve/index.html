What the app is

Evolve is a cross-platform Chronoflux workshop. It is not a mining pool and not a chain explorer. It is the place you ask “what is the chance of…?” and get a local, inspectable answer.

%

Percent chance

Calibrated ~% headlines with regressive / progressive lean, continuum conclusion, and the historical cases that formed the base rate.

SCS

Social cohesion

Full PART ONE / TWO / THREE reports. Paste a narrative link, score attributed quotes, and watch cohesion move.

5

Five actions

Every run ends with five numbered actions for the accountable figurehead — mayor, minister, agency lead — plus copy and export.

⌘

Local first

All core Chronoflux calculations run on your device. No account is required to analyse a scenario offline.

✶

Optional Grok

Slide GROK CONSTRUE to fill blank σ / Iτ / Jμ / ω fields. Fields you already typed are never overwritten.

FCG

Community governance

A quantitative companion to Full Community Governance — ward debate, SSUCF cohesion voting, and PERC parish tools in one suite.

How a run feels

Same pipeline on every platform. Region first, question second, constructs only if you want to bias the flow.

1
Pick a region and language.
Scopes observations and construct language. Not Global unless you choose Global.
2
Pose the question (ω).
“What is the chance of sporadic civil unrest near-term?” Optional shear, resistance, and flow sit underneath.
3
Calculate.
Local Chronoflux continuum, then five establishment actions. Export PDF, Markdown, or copy the synopsis.
4
Read the honesty layer.
Every conclusion lists the registry filter, horizon, and each OR-xxxx case used in the base rate.
