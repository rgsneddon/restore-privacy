How PERC shares confirm

Perccent PERC pool · 72 minutes until a pool-found block is spendable

Miners submit hashes to the pool. A valid hash that meets job work is an accepted share. The first accepted solution at a height is the found block. That block stays unconfirmed until 72 minutes have passed. After 72 minutes it is confirmed and the PERC mined at the pool is spendable from the wallet.
